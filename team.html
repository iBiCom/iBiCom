Lantian Yao received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from The Chinese University of Hong Kong in 2025. He is currently an Assistant Professor (特聘副教授) and Ph.D. Supervisor at Shenzhen University of Advanced Technology. His research focuses on the interdisciplinary field of artificial intelligence and bioinformatics, with research interests spanning bioinformatics, machine learning and deep learning, biomedical big data analysis, AI-assisted drug design, and precision medicine. His work aims to develop novel computational and AI methods to address important scientific problems in life sciences and medicine.

In recent years, he has conducted systematic research in bioinformatics, computational biology, and AI-assisted drug design. He has published more than 40 peer-reviewed papers, including over 30 as a first or corresponding author (including co-first and co-corresponding authors). His work has appeared in leading journals in these fields, including Nucleic Acids Research, Briefings in Bioinformatics, and Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling, with more than 2,300 total citations. He also serves as a reviewer and Associate Editor for international journals, including Nucleic Acids Research, Genome Biology, Genome Research, and Microbiome.
